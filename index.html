    Niner Scheduler

    Niner Scheduler is an independent schedule planner for UNC Charlotte students. Not affiliated with or endorsed by UNC Charlotte.

    Your privacy

    No accounts, cookies, analytics or ads. Courses, preferences and saved schedules are stored locally in your browser.

    Privacy policy

    About the data

    Sections come from UNC Charlotte's public class search and refresh every couple of hours. Grade distributions come from the Office of the Registrar.

    Banner registration

    How it works

    1. Add your courses. Search by code, title or CRN. Typing a CRN adds the course with that section locked in.
    2. Narrow it down. Lock a section you want, untick sections or whole professors you don't, and set days off, earliest and latest times, and breaks. Drag on the calendar to block off time for work or practice.
    3. Browse every schedule. Every combination without a time conflict is generated instantly. Sort by fewest days, least time between classes, best-rated professors, highest average GPA and more. Use ← and → to flip through them.
    4. Register. Copy the CRNs into Banner's “Enter CRNs” tab, add the schedule to your calendar, or share a link with friends.

    A few extras

    • Optional courses. Mark a course optional and set a credit range to get schedules that include whichever extras fit.
    • Linked sections. Lectures that need a lab or recitation are paired automatically, using Banner's link groups.
    • 8-week courses. First-half and second-half sections at the same time don't count as a conflict.
    • Compare. Save schedules, open one to load its courses back into your plan, view two side by side, or overlay a friend's shared schedule to find time together.
    • Why nothing fits. When no schedule works, the board tells you which course or which pair of courses is the problem.

    Section times and seats come from UNC Charlotte's public class search, refreshed every couple of hours. Grade distributions come from UNC Charlotte's Office of the Registrar, and professor ratings from Rate My Professors. Your plan and saved schedules stay in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
